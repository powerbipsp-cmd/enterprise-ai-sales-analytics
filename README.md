# Enterprise AI Sales Analytics

## Business problem

A fictional global retailer needs a reliable view of sales, margin,
customers, products, and regional performance. Leaders also want
plain-language explanations based on validated business metrics.

## Intended users

Sales leaders, finance managers, and regional managers.

## Questions to answer

- How are revenue and margin changing over time?
- Which regions and products drive those changes?
- How do actual sales compare with targets?
- What does each KPI mean?

## Planned solution

Prepare data with SQL and Python, build a dimensional model and Power BI
dashboard, then add grounded AI summaries and business-glossary Q&A.

## Data

This project will use synthetic sample data, not employer or client data.
